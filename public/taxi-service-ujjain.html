Taxi Service in Ujjain for Local Rides and Outstation Trips

Planning a city ride, a visit to Mahakaleshwar Temple or a journey beyond Ujjain? Send Divine Travels your pickup, destination and travel date to discuss vehicle availability and a confirmed fare.

Taxi options in Ujjain

Divine Travels accepts enquiries for local taxi travel, one-way journeys, round trips, temple visits and outstation routes. Share your itinerary before booking so the team can confirm whether the requested trip is available.

Plan a Mahakaleshwar Temple visit

If Mahakaleshwar Temple is part of your itinerary, allow time for your planned darshan, local traffic and any other places you want to visit. Temple entry arrangements and schedules can change, so confirm current details through official temple information before travelling.

How to request a taxi

  1. Share your pickup point and destination.
  2. Provide your travel date, preferred pickup time and whether the trip is one-way or a return journey.
  3. Tell the team your passenger count and preferred vehicle.
  4. Confirm the total fare, inclusions and availability before finalising the booking.

Vehicle options and fares

The main website lists indicative per-kilometre rates for Swift Dzire, Ertiga and Innova Crysta. Final trip cost can depend on distance, minimum billing, driver allowance, tolls, parking, waiting time and other applicable charges. Ask for a complete fare confirmation for your route.

Frequently asked questions

Can I enquire about a one-way taxi from Ujjain?

Yes. Share your destination and travel date so Divine Travels can confirm availability and pricing for your route.

Can I book a taxi for local sightseeing?

You can enquire about local rides and visits to nearby destinations. Provide your planned stops and expected duration so the team can advise on availability and fare.

How do I confirm my booking?

Call or message Divine Travels with your trip details. Your booking is confirmed only after the team agrees the route, vehicle, timing and fare.
